## Who it is for

Learners who already understand how to build AI agentic workflows and want to develop the mindset of an AI Solutions Architect and Developer. The course uses n8n as its reference workflow tool; if you come from Make, Zapier, Copilot Studio or similar tools, the same concepts apply.

## Why harness design matters

On the same coding tasks, changing only the harness moved benchmark scores by 27.4 points, against 29.4 points for changing the model (Claw-SWE-Bench, June 2026, as reported in Vizuara's Harness Engineering book). The scaffolding around the model is an engineering decision, and this course teaches it.

## How each week runs

The reading is posted here three days before each 2-hour live session. The session discusses the ideas; the assignment (core steps for everyone, stretch steps if you have time) is done at your own pace, and a self-check tells you it works. Nothing is submitted and nothing is graded: you leave with a portfolio you built and can explain. Every week carries four threads: a **security lens**, an **architect's corner** with a short ADR, a **break it** challenge and an **n8n bridge**.
